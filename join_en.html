Graduate students

Graduate students

We are looking for graduate students who want to study gametogenesis and cellular rejuvenation in budding yeast. Besides wet-lab biology, students interested in microscope image analysis or programming are welcome. You can join us through the following programs. Please check each program's website for admission details.

What you will learn

Skills you will gain

  • Yeast genetics and molecular biology

    Learn the core techniques for studying genes and proteins through genetic manipulation of budding yeast and analysis of mutants.

  • Live-cell imaging

    Observe membranes and organelles labeled with fluorescent proteins and follow changes inside living cells over time.

  • Image analysis and deep learning

    Quantify microscope images with ImageJ and deep learning, turning visual impressions into numbers.
