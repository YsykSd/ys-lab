Getting here

  1. Take the Tsukuba Express rapid service from Akihabara to Tsukuba Station (about 45 minutes).

  2. At the Tsukuba Center bus terminal next to the station, take the “Tsukuba Daigaku Junkan (clockwise)” bus.

  3. Get off at “Tsukuba Daigaku Byoin Higashi” or “Oikoshi Gakusei Shukusha-mae” (about 10 minutes).
